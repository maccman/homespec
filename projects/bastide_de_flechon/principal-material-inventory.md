Native files were opened before this inventory; photo55 contains different light/staging and is supporting material evidence rather than an exposure target. Existing shader/UV implementation inspected in fidelity_materials.py, fidelity_timbers.py and fidelity_bedrooms.py. No source material or geometry changed before this inventory.

1. **High: curtain repeat.** Photograph33 shows a rust red and unbleached ivory ikat with large irregular linked diamonds, small nested lozenges, feathered/broken edges, uneven ochre faded patches. Existing rust_ikat plus repeated 0.8/1.65 mapping produces mechanically repeating bright zigzags. Make a dedicated color-only albedo, map in unfolded cloth metres, preserve real pleats and independently shallow yarn normal. The source is contrasty but folds/wear soften it; avoid simply flattening all contrast.
2. **High: timber pigment and variation.** Photos06/33 show mixed pale honey, oxidized brown, knots/checks and locally washed surfaces, with much darker backs/shadows. The current one shared reclaimed_oak treatment is too dark/regular. Use a principal-specific longitudinal shader with bounded faded/sapwood color variation, preserve existing metric member UVs and distinct endgrain slots, and leave existing inward ageing geometry intact. Do not brighten all house beams.
3. **High: side table.** Photo33 trumpet is dark brown/charcoal with mottled, rubbed bronze highlights and a narrow reflected highlight. Existing shared flechon_bronze is light smooth gold. Create separate mottled pigment and broader independent roughness variation; keep physical Fresnel. Photos06 and55 use a scalloped shiny drum table instead, so no single table material can match all three.
4. **Medium: chairs/cushions.** Photo33 chair frames are dark brown near black with faded warm arrises; cane has fine warm openings. Seats are softly wrinkled warm ivory/olive, not saturated green. Photo06 shadows and highlights vary. Dedicated room materials prevent recoloring the other bedrooms.
5. **Medium: bed cover.** Photo06/55 use fine gray/ivory botanical-paisley motifs on a charcoal field, bordered and fringed at the foot. A reddish band in photo06 is direct sunlight, not evidence for a red dyed stripe. Existing principal_paisley is retained as the base asset; first soften saturation and adjust pigment only, without using motif luminance as relief.
6. **Medium: floorboards.** Photos show varied medium brown/gray oak with bright shallow reflections, individual joints and grain. Existing floor shader roughness .87 is very matte and dark. Scoped principal floor finish is needed, but room boundary and floor sharing must be checked before assignment; individual joint/board geometry remains root-owned.
7. **Medium: plaster.** Source33 shows broad hand-applied ochre/tobacco strokes; source06 is much dimmer and source55 warmer. Existing tobacco albedo contains useful stroke variation. Keep existing material masking on inward upper wall faces and never recolor the lower salon/exterior. Root should assess under neutral and photo light before any broad color shift.
8. **Low: ceramics/blind/bedding.** Vessels are fine matte pale ivory ceramic, not visibly woven fabric. Blind is loosely woven warm off-white; bedsheet mostly cream, with folds carrying shading. Dedicated ceramic and linen roughness/microrelief are useful and do not require generated scans.

Uncertainty: unknown camera white balance/exposure, spatially varying sunlight, no measured reflectance scan, no evidence for exact roughness values.
